This section contains the established human and clinical supports that form Project Valens's evidence-based intervention foundation. These materials are intentionally separated from:

  • AI-associated case research;
  • proposed Valens product safeguards;
  • experimental detection and prevention concepts;
  • media reports and litigation.

Core evidence review

Read the full Proven Supports evidence review.

It covers:

  • coordinated specialty care and early-intervention services;
  • clinician-managed medication continuity and shared decision-making;
  • family intervention;
  • cognitive behavioral therapy for psychosis;
  • sleep assessment and evidence-based insomnia treatment;
  • psychoeducation and individualized relapse planning;
  • supported employment and education;
  • integrated substance-use assessment and care;
  • outcome evidence from the published AI-associated clinical cases;
  • direct implications and boundaries for Project Valens.

Evidence boundary

These supports have evidence for psychosis, first-episode psychosis, relapse prevention, sleep, functioning, or recovery. They have not yet been proven specifically to prevent AI-associated psychosis or delusional spiraling. Valens can use them as the human-care foundation while separately testing its product-level safeguards.
